# ProductCard

One product on the home page's grid: a tag, the product's name, one or two lines, a detail link, and its art.

- A `paper` card with the `radius` corner and a `line` border. The whole card is the link; on hover it rises 3px on `st-lift`.
- The tag uses capitals in `ring`. The name is `heading-3`, a little larger. The detail link is `label-link` in `accent`.
- Genie's card shows Genie. **The only Genie is the full-body one** (the Genie asset group); never the head alone.
- Other cards hold a capture of the real app in a window or phone frame. Those captures belong to the site, not to this system.
